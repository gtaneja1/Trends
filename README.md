
AI-Powered Sales & Marketing Intelligence Platform

## Overview
Helping small businesses make smarter marketing decisions through AI-powered customer insights, sentiment analysis, and actionable recommendations.

The AI-Powered Sales & Marketing Intelligence Platform is a product designed to help small businesses make informed marketing decisions using AI-driven insights. The platform analyzes customer sentiment, market trends, and campaign performance to provide actionable recommendations for improving engagement and business growth.
Overview

Small businesses generate valuable customer feedback through reviews, surveys, emails, and social media—but analyzing this data often requires multiple tools and significant manual effort.

The AI-Powered Sales & Marketing Intelligence Platform centralizes customer sentiment analysis, campaign performance, and market insights into a single dashboard, enabling business owners to make faster, data-driven marketing decisions.

---
This project was built with a product-first approach, combining customer research, AI, and interactive analytics to simplify decision-making for small businesses.

## Problem
The Problem

Many small businesses lack access to affordable analytics tools that combine customer feedback, market trends, and AI-powered recommendations into one platform.
During customer discovery interviews, creators and small business owners consistently described the same challenges:

Business owners often rely on multiple disconnected tools to understand:
- Customer sentiment
- Marketing performance
- Competitive trends
- Campaign effectiveness
Customer feedback was spread across multiple platforms.
Manual review analysis was time-consuming.
Existing analytics tools were expensive or overly complex.
Businesses wanted recommendations, not just raw metrics.

Our goal was to create a centralized platform that simplifies decision-making.
These insights shaped the product vision and guided feature prioritization for the MVP.

Customer Discovery

## Product Goals
Before designing the product, I conducted 10+ interviews with creators, influencers, and small business owners to better understand their marketing workflows.

- Help businesses understand customer sentiment.
- Identify marketing opportunities using AI.
- Reduce manual analysis through automation.
- Present insights in an intuitive dashboard.
- Support data-driven business decisions.


Key Findings
Businesses struggled to consolidate customer feedback.
Most manually reviewed comments and online reviews.
Simple dashboards were preferred over feature-heavy analytics tools.
Actionable recommendations provided more value than raw data alone.

These findings were translated into user stories, product requirements, and an MVP roadmap.

## Customer Discovery

Conducted interviews with **10+ creators, influencers, and small business owners** to understand:

- Biggest marketing challenges
- Current workflow
- Existing tools
- Pain points
- Desired features

### Key Insights

- Businesses struggled to consolidate customer feedback.
- Most relied on manual analysis of reviews and social media.
- Users preferred simple dashboards over complex analytics.

These findings were translated into product requirements and prioritized into the MVP roadmap.
